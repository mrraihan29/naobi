# 03 — Target Audience, Personas, and Jobs-to-be-Done

**Document status:** Draft v0.1  
**Validation note:** Personas are evidence-informed hypotheses derived from the
Product Owner interview. External user validation was declined and is recorded
as an accepted risk.

## Audience hierarchy

### Primary site-wide audience

Professionals in their respective fields, especially AI research, engineering,
cost engineering, and project estimation.

### AI Research environment

1. AI researchers
2. General readers interested in AI
3. Open-source community

### Engineering environment

1. Cost engineers
2. Engineering students
3. Prospective clients

## Persona hypotheses

### Persona A — AI Research Professional

**Context:** Researcher or research engineer evaluating whether Raihan's work is
interesting and credible.

**Needs:**

- concise research question and contribution;
- methodology, target, data, baseline, evaluation, and limitations;
- clarity about what is observed, inferred, and speculative;
- reproducibility artifacts and version information;
- a fast path to code, report, or repository.

**Failure conditions:**

- impressive visuals hide weak evidence;
- neuroscience language implies more than the model demonstrates;
- no baseline or evaluation design is visible;
- an interactive demo cannot be connected to the research claim.

### Persona B — Cost Engineering Professional

**Context:** Estimator, cost engineer, or engineering professional assessing
Raihan's technical discipline.

**Needs:**

- scope and project context without client disclosure;
- basis, assumptions, source hierarchy, and methods;
- traceable reasoning and validation;
- clear boundary between public example and proprietary database;
- lessons that are transferable to other work.

**Failure conditions:**

- client or proprietary data leaks;
- polished totals appear without a basis;
- a case study overclaims responsibility or commercial outcome;
- AI-style language obscures engineering specifics.

### Persona C — Prospective Project Owner

**Context:** A person or organization with an AI or engineering project who is
considering collaboration.

**Needs:**

- understand capability and fit quickly;
- see credible examples;
- know what information to provide;
- select email, structured inquiry, professional profile, repository, or future
  scheduling option;
- receive clear confirmation that an inquiry was sent.

**Failure conditions:**

- collaboration call-to-action is buried;
- project types are vague;
- inquiry form is intrusive or excessively long;
- no clear next step exists.

### Persona D — Open-source Contributor

**Context:** Developer or researcher who wants to inspect, reuse, discuss, or
contribute to an open artifact.

**Needs:**

- repository, documentation, license, versions, issues, and contribution route;
- clear distinction between open and closed artifacts;
- reproducibility instructions;
- technical context and known limitations.

### Persona E — General or Student Reader

**Context:** Curious non-specialist, AI reader, or engineering student.

**Needs:**

- accessible summary before advanced detail;
- terms and equations explained in context;
- visuals that develop intuition;
- optional deeper layers without forcing them;
- related reading and clear orientation.

## Jobs-to-be-Done

| ID | Job |
|---|---|
| JTBD-001 | When I first encounter Raihan, help me understand his professional identity and choose the relevant domain quickly. |
| JTBD-002 | When I assess a research claim, let me inspect the method, evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and artifacts without losing my place. |
| JTBD-003 | When I assess engineering capability, show defensible reasoning and safe evidence without exposing confidential material. |
| JTBD-004 | When a concept is difficult, let me manipulate a meaningful model or visualization to build intuition. |
| JTBD-005 | When I only need an overview, let me understand the work without reading the full technical detail. |
| JTBD-006 | When I want to reproduce or extend open work, take me directly to the correct repository, report, data, and license. |
| JTBD-007 | When I have a project, help me determine fit and start a useful collaboration conversation. |
| JTBD-008 | When I return for new thinking, help me find recent research, essays, notes, and revisions. |

## Accessibility and inclusion implication

Progressive disclosure is not permission to hide required evidence. The
accessible summary, technical depth, and interactive material are different
views of one content object. Every core destination must remain reachable
through visual navigation, search, and stable direct URLs.
