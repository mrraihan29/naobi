# 18 — Product Requirements Document

**Product:** Raihan's OS  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft for Product Owner walkthrough  
**Product Owner / Project Manager:** Raihan  
**Prepared with:** Rei

## 1. Executive summary

Raihan's OS is a desktop-first personal research and engineering publication
environment. It introduces Raihan briefly through a Central OS and gives
visitors two primary gateways: AI Research and Engineering/Mining Project
Estimation. Both environments are visually distinct but share one functional
system shell, terminal, search, reading mode, Blog/Journal, About/CV, and
Collaboration Center.

The product is intended to impress first and then earn trust through
methodology, source transparency, evidence, calibrated claims, limitations,
reproducibility, and confidentiality-safe professional cases.

## 2. Why now

Raihan has substantial interests and work across AI, research, engineering, and
mining estimation but lacks a public environment that communicates those
interests as one coherent identity. A conventional portfolio would introduce
Raihan but would not provide a living home for original research, interactive
explanation, personal writing, professional evidence, and future collaboration.

## 3. Vision

Create a digital environment that makes a visitor say, “This is Raihan,” then
allows that visitor to inspect the thinking and evidence beneath the identity.

## 4. Product outcomes

- Original research corpus.
- Interactive and readable technical communication.
- Broad English-first Blog/Journal.
- Professional reputation across AI and engineering.
- Qualified research and project collaboration.
- Durable intellectual and research lineage.

## 5. Priority hierarchy

1. Quality and rigor
2. Visual experience
3. Maintainability
4. Originality
5. Speed

Mandatory accessibility, privacy, security, confidentiality, and scientific
integrity requirements are gates rather than weighted preferences.

## 6. Users

### Primary

- AI research professionals
- Cost engineering and engineering professionals
- Prospective project owners

### Secondary

- General AI readers
- Open-source contributors

See Document 03 for personas and jobs.

## 7. Value propositions

### Site-wide

A personal research and engineering environment where ideas are not merely
presented, but exposed through methodology, evidence, experimentation, and
transparent results.

### AI Research

Original and open or inspectable research presented through accessible
explanation, interactive evidence, technical depth, and reproducible artifacts
where available.

### Engineering

Rigorous engineering and mining cost-estimation work grounded in methodology,
traceable sources, quantitative reasoning, transparent assumptions, and
confidentiality-safe evidence.

## 8. Experience concept

### Central OS

- Short introduction to Raihan.
- Immediate AI and Engineering gateways.
- Flagship, recent work, and collaboration entry.

### Shared shell

- stable identity/home;
- current environment;
- environment switcher;
- app launcher/dock;
- terminal;
- search;
- reading mode;
- Journal;
- About/CV;
- Collaboration Center.

### Signature capabilities

- skippable boot sequence;
- functional terminal;
- environment/theme switching;
- distraction-reduced reading mode;
- at least one guided interactive launch flagship.

The OS metaphor must be functional. Every core command has a visual
equivalent, every content object has a stable URL, and direct-linked content
does not require a boot ritual.

## 9. Content

### Launch inventory

- one interactive flagship research/project;

- two essays;
- About/CV;
- controlled Coming Soon entries.

### Types

- Research
- Experiment
- Project
- Engineering Case Study

### Current candidate inventory

- Sentiment Classification with BERT
- Predictive Maintenance for Solar Filter with Machine Learning
- Hermes Affective Core
- Hermes Emotion with Synthetic Hormone System
- selected mining estimator projects
- CV and professional experience

Classification remains provisional until artifacts are audited.

### Research trust model

    Claim
      → Methodology
      → Context and source/data basis
      → Result and uncertainty
      → Limitations/failure
      → Supporting artifact

Research pages separate design, observation, estimate, inference,
interpretation, speculation, and limitation. Neuroscience-inspired content
calibrates biological and engineering claims.

### Confidentiality

No client names or proprietary estimator database content. Each engineering
case passes a redaction, hidden-metadata, source, role, and claim review.

## 10. Functional summary

The system must:

- operate as one OS with two environments;
- provide terminal and visual navigation;
- provide search, filtering, stable URLs, and related content;
- render structured technical and editorial content;
- support progressive depth and interaction tiers;
- publish through a version-controlled single-author workflow;
- display version, evidence, status, and license;
- provide collaboration form and alternate channels;
- support English at launch and future translated URLs;
- provide a full desktop and simpler functional mobile experience;
- omit comments, visitor accounts, and cross-visit preference history.

Detailed requirements: Document 09.

## 11. Non-functional summary

- WCAG 2.2 Level AA target plus reduced-motion behavior.
- Core Web Vitals targets: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms, CLS ≤0.1 at the
  75th percentile for primary routes.
- Privacy-first aggregate analytics.
- HTTPS, secure headers, validated/rate-limited forms, dependency review.
- Separate staging/production, rollback, backups, restoration test, monitoring.
- Crawlable semantic content and direct URLs.
- Maintainable content-as-code workflow.
- Current stable major-browser support and responsive content.

Detailed requirements: Document 10.

## 12. Authoring and governance

Recommended baseline:

- version-controlled Markdown/MDX-like content;
- structured metadata;
- reusable content and interaction components;
- staging preview;
- automated metadata/link validation;
- editorial, research, confidentiality, licensing, accessibility, and visual
  gates;
- explicit Raihan approval.

English is published first. Target cadence is at least one meaningful update
per month. Raihan is the sole site author.

## 13. Privacy, analytics, and SEO

- no advertising or visitor behavioral profiles;
- no fingerprinting or cross-site tracking;
- minimum inquiry data;
- no form contents in analytics;
- published content is indexable;
- stable URLs, crawlable links, metadata, sitemap, structured data;
- future language versions use separate URLs and correct language metadata.

Detailed policy: Document 12.

## 14. Release strategy

There is no public beta.

1. Phase 1 sign-off.
2. Private platform foundation.
3. Private platform-complete milestone.
4. Flagship and minimum launch content.
5. Repeated QC and regression cycles.
6. Production smoke testing.
7. Explicit Product Owner approval.
8. Polished official launch.

## 15. Validation strategy

The Product Owner declined external pre-launch user testing and accepts the
representation risk. Validation uses:

- Product Owner task observation;
- heuristic review;
- requirements traceability;
- content and evidence audit;
- accessibility review;
- performance and security testing;
- responsive and browser testing;
- backup/recovery drill;
- repeated regression and visual QC.

Post-launch aggregate analytics will expose navigation and content problems
without visitor profiling.

## 16. Major risks

- OS theme is no longer unique by itself.
- Flagship does not yet exist.
- interactive content can become expensive to author;
- visuals can harm performance/readability;
- two domains can fragment identity;
- internal-only testing can miss novice problems;
- mining material can leak confidential data;
- external services can violate privacy;
- JavaScript shell can impair indexing;
- license ambiguity can undermine reuse.

Detailed register and mitigations: Document 13.
