# 05 — Scope, Out of Scope, and MVP Boundary

**Document status:** Draft v0.1

## Minimum launch product

The Product Owner approved the following launch content baseline:

- Central Raihan's OS experience
- AI Research environment
- Engineering and Mining environment
- About and CV
- Collaboration Center
- one flagship interactive research or project publication
- one confidentiality-safe engineering case study
- two published blog essays
- controlled Coming Soon entries

The official launch is blocked until this baseline and all quality gates pass.

## Must-have launch scope

### Experience

- short, skippable boot sequence;
- central OS introduction and two visible gateways;
- persistent shared system shell;
- distinct AI and Engineering environments;
- easy environment switching;
- functional interactive terminal;
- visual equivalent for every terminal navigation action;
- theme/environment switching;
- reading mode;
- global search and direct URLs;
- desktop-first full experience;
- simplified but functional mobile experience.

### Content

- Research, Experiment, Project, Engineering Case Study, Blog Essay, Note, and
  Coming Soon content models;
- progressive disclosure;
- code, equations, citations, diagrams, tables, and evidence links;
- content status, dates, versions, and licenses;
- English-first canonical publication;
- open/closed status per artifact;
- monthly update capability.

### Research and engineering evidence

- research quality and reproducibility gate;
- claim and limitation labeling;
- negative-result support;
- confidentiality review for mining content;
- no client identity or proprietary estimator database disclosure.

### Collaboration

- Collaboration Center;
- structured project inquiry;
- direct email option;
- professional/social link options;
- GitHub route for technical and open-source work;
- success, validation-error, and delivery-failure states.

### Quality

- WCAG 2.2 Level AA target, plus reduced-motion behavior;
- Core Web Vitals targets;
- privacy-first analytics;
- HTTPS and application-security controls;
- SEO, sitemap, structured metadata, and crawlable links;
- repeated functional, visual, content, security, accessibility, performance,
  and cross-device QC.

## Should-have scope

- Research Ledger data model and visible lineage when enough content exists;
- research timeline;
- downloadable reports;
- RSS or equivalent feed;
- related-content recommendations based on declared metadata;
- research graph when it conveys meaningful relationships;
- status/current-focus module;
- reusable interactive-component library;
- automated content-schema and link validation.

## Could-have scope

- advanced semantic search;
- advanced visual knowledge graph;
- scheduling integration;
- additional environment themes;
- richer mobile OS behavior;
- automatic repository metadata refresh;
- translated versions of selected content;
- downloadable offline research package.

## Explicitly out of scope for first release

- public comments;
- visitor accounts or profiles;
- remembered theme, language, environment, or reading position across visits;
- multi-author CMS accounts;
- public work-in-progress research;
- automatic full-site translation;
- a complete desktop-window metaphor on mobile;
- advertising, behavioral profiling, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking;
- payment or commerce;
- publication of client names, confidential prices, vendor quotes, or the
  proprietary estimator database;
- unrestricted server-side execution of visitor-supplied code;
- a public beta release.

## Boundary conditions

### Platform completion versus launch

The platform can reach private platform-complete status before a flagship
exists. Public launch cannot occur until the flagship is created, integrated,
reviewed, and validated.

### Research Ledger and graph

The information model must support lineage at launch. A graph should not be
shown as empty theater. It becomes visible only when relationships among
hypotheses, experiments, failures, and follow-up work are meaningful.

### Interactivity

Not every page must contain a custom simulation. Every technical page receives
the highest useful interaction tier that improves understanding without
creating disproportionate maintenance cost.
