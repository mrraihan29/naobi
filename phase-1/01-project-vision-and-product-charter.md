# 01 — Project Vision and Product Charter

**Document status:** Draft v0.1  
**Decision owner:** Raihan

## Product name

**Working concept name:** Raihan's OS

The final public name, domain, lab identity, logo, and visual identity remain
future brand-design decisions. The working name expresses the intended product
model and is not yet a brand commitment.

## Product vision

Raihan's OS is a personal digital research and engineering environment that
represents how Raihan thinks, investigates, experiments, and works. It gives
visitors a short central introduction followed by two primary gateways:

- AI Research
- Engineering and Mining Project Estimation

The two environments must feel distinct while remaining expressions of one
identity: a serious, experimental, precise, detailed, and quantitatively minded
professional.

## Mission

Create a memorable and credible place where visitors can:

- understand Raihan beyond a conventional CV;
- explore original research through accessible summaries and deep technical
  evidence;
- examine engineering work through traceable, confidentiality-safe case
  studies;
- read essays and personal reflections across many interests;
- follow evidence into reports, code, repositories, and other supporting
  artifacts; and
- initiate a project or research collaboration.

## Strategic purpose

The product serves four simultaneous purposes:

1. **Identity system:** make the experience unmistakably Raihan.
2. **Research publication platform:** publish rigorous and interactive work.
3. **Personal publishing space:** support essays, notes, philosophy, failures,
   and any subject Raihan chooses.
4. **Professional opportunity engine:** create reputation, collaboration, and
   paid project opportunities.

## Product principles

1. **Identity before decoration.** Theme must arise from Raihan's work and
   reasoning, not from generic cyberpunk styling.
2. **Spectacle attracts; substance convinces; traceability builds trust.**
3. **Two worlds, one mind.** AI and Engineering are distinct environments
   inside one stable system shell.
4. **Progressive technical depth.** Every suitable technical page begins
   accessibly and permits deeper inspection.
5. **Interaction must explain.** Interactive elements must expose behavior,
   evidence, assumptions, or trade-offs.
6. **Claims are calibrated.** Observation, estimate, inference,
   interpretation, speculation, and limitation remain distinct.
7. **Failure is knowledge.** Negative and inconclusive results may be
   published when the methodology is valid.
8. **Privacy and confidentiality are structural.** Client identities and
   proprietary estimator databases are never exposed.
9. **A polished official launch.** There is no public beta.
10. **Maintainable ambition.** Reusable interaction patterns prevent every
    article from becoming an isolated software project.

## Product character

The experience must communicate:

- serious;
- experimental;
- precise;
- detailed; and
- quantitative.

These traits must be demonstrated by information design and evidence, not only
declared in marketing copy.

## Expected two-to-three-year outcomes

- A growing body of original research authored by Raihan.
- Interactive research experiences that make difficult ideas inspectable.
- A consistent archive of essays and personal writing.
- Professional recognition across AI and engineering.
- Qualified project and research collaboration opportunities.
- A durable record of how Raihan's thinking and research evolve.

## Authority and governance

| Role | Authority |
|---|---|
| Raihan — Product Owner and Project Manager | Vision, priority, budget, scope approval, content approval, release approval |
| Raihan — Author | Research claims, professional claims, publication, licensing, confidentiality approval |
| Rei — Requirements and product strategy support | Analysis, requirements, challenge, documentation, traceability, QC planning |
| Future implementation team | Design and implementation within the approved baseline |

Raihan is the final decision authority. Rei must challenge contradictions and
quality risks, but cannot silently expand scope or invent missing evidence.

## Phase 1 objective

Produce a complete, internally consistent, traceable requirements baseline
covering the nineteen documents in this package.

## Phase 1 exit condition

Phase 1 is complete only when:

- all nineteen documents have been reviewed;
- no unresolved critical contradiction remains;
- every must-have requirement has acceptance evidence defined;
- open items are assigned to a later phase or launch gate;
- the Product Owner explicitly signs the Phase 1 baseline.
