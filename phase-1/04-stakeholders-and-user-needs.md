## Validation approach

The Product Owner declined external user testing for the initial phase and will
act as the primary quality observer. This decision does not remove usability
verification. It changes the validation method to:

- task-based Product Owner observation;
- heuristic UX review;
- keyboard-only review;
- screen-reader and semantic review;
- reduced-motion review;
- responsive and cross-browser review;
- content comprehension review at summary and technical levels;
- repeated regression testing.

The limitation is recorded in Risk RSK-005: internal observers may not
represent unfamiliar visitors.
