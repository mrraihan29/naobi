# 02 — Problem Statement and Product Goals

**Document status:** Draft v0.1

## Core problem

Raihan has strong interests and work across AI research, quantitative
engineering, and mining project estimation, but lacks one public environment
that combines those fields into a coherent and memorable identity. A generic
portfolio would show outputs while failing to express how Raihan thinks.

## Problem statements

| ID | Problem |
|---|---|
| P-001 | Raihan's professional identity is distributed across different domains and artifacts. |
| P-002 | Conventional portfolio structures cannot adequately express Raihan's desired persona or experimental character. |
| P-003 | Static research pages can make technical work feel passive, opaque, or inaccessible. |
| P-004 | AI and mining estimation attract different audiences and risk fragmenting the brand. |
| P-005 | Visitors may see a result without understanding its methodology, sources, limitations, or reproducibility. |
| P-006 | Potential collaborators lack a clear, qualified path to propose projects. |
| P-007 | Research failures, changes of mind, and intellectual lineage have no durable public home. |
| P-008 | Confidential mining work cannot be shown safely without a deliberate case-study and redaction model. |

## Product goals

| ID | Goal | Evidence of achievement |
|---|---|---|
| G-001 | Establish a distinctive and coherent public identity. | Visitors can identify Raihan's domains and defining traits from the central experience. |
| G-002 | Present AI and Engineering as two strong environments inside one identity. | Both gateways are clear, switchable, and visibly connected by one system shell. |
| G-003 | Publish research with progressive technical depth. | A page supports summary, method, evidence, limitations, and supporting artifacts. |
| G-004 | Make suitable research actively explorable. | At least one launch flagship uses interaction to inspect assumptions, behavior, or evidence. |
| G-005 | Build professional trust through transparency. | Claims link to methodology, sources, results, limitations, and evidence as applicable. |
| G-006 | Support broad personal writing without weakening information architecture. | Blog/Journal accepts multiple topics and remains searchable and classifiable. |
| G-007 | Generate qualified collaboration opportunities. | Visitors can choose an appropriate collaboration channel and provide useful project context. |
| G-008 | Preserve confidentiality and licensing clarity. | Every published artifact passes confidentiality and license gates. |
| G-009 | Remain maintainable by one author. | Raihan can publish at least one meaningful update per month using the approved workflow. |
| G-010 | Deliver a polished official launch. | All launch gates and repeated QC cycles pass before public release. |

## Non-goals

The product is not intended to:

- imitate a desktop operating system solely for novelty;
- operate as a social network or public discussion forum;
- publish confidential client data or the proprietary estimator database;
- present unfinished work as completed research;
- claim academic or scientific authority beyond the evidence;
- serve as a generic recruiter-first résumé template;
- require visitors to learn terminal commands before accessing content;
- support multiple site-author accounts in the first release.

## Product outcome metrics

Numeric business targets will be baselined after launch because no historical
traffic exists. The product will track trends rather than invent pre-launch
targets.

| Dimension | Metric |
|---|---|
| Publishing | At least one meaningful content update per month |
| Discovery | Entry into AI, Engineering, Blog, and flagship content |
| Depth | Movement from summary to methodology, evidence, repository, or report |
| Collaboration | Qualified collaboration starts and completed inquiries |
| Open research | Outbound repository visits and artifact downloads |
| Content quality | Editorial, evidence, confidentiality, and license gates passed |
| Experience quality | Accessibility, performance, security, and usability gates passed |
| Reliability | Availability, error rate, backup, and recovery checks |

Analytics must remain aggregate and privacy-first. Metrics must not require
cross-site tracking, fingerprinting, or personal visitor profiles.

## Product success statement

Raihan's OS succeeds when a professional visitor is first impressed, then
curious, then convinced by evidence, and finally motivated to explore or
collaborate—without sacrificing readability, accessibility, privacy, or
credibility.
