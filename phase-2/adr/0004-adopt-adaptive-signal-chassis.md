# ADR-0004 — Adopt Adaptive Signal Chassis

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Decision owner:** Raihan

## Context

Gate 2.2 compared three visual-system studies derived from the accepted Quiet
Field Console foundation and Interview 2B. Raihan approved Study A and its
design-lead recommendations. The system now needs one identity baseline before
component anatomy, low-fidelity flows, and high-fidelity prototypes can be
specified consistently.

## Decision drivers

- fixed near-black identity with visitor-selectable signal character;
- one chassis across AI Research and Engineering;
- structured density without ornamental telemetry;
- precise and direct interface voice;
- a strong signature interaction that is not a fictional-device imitation;
- privacy-first self-hosted typography;
- accessible meaning independent of color; and
- bounded theme complexity and maintainability.

## Considered options

### Study A — Adaptive Signal Chassis

Selected. It best preserves the personal instrument metaphor while separating
signal profile, environment behavior, and evidence depth into controlled axes.

### Study B — Technical Archive Console

Not selected as the base. It offers excellent reading conditions but risks
making the OS a decorative wrapper around an editorial website.

### Study C — Dense Operations Matrix

Not selected as the base. It offers strong scale and spectacle but has the
highest cyberpunk, navigation, responsive, and maintenance risk.

## Decision

- Adopt **Adaptive Signal Chassis** as the shared identity baseline.
- Keep Void, Carbon, Alloy, Instrument Text, and Muted Telemetry fixed.
- Offer Phosphor, Amber, and Ice as selectable signal profiles.
- Keep semantic status independent from the selected signal profile.
- Use Recursive Linear for system/UI/utility roles and Source Serif 4 for
  intentional editorial contexts, subject to prototype verification.
- Use an angular modular structure, restrained rounding, one controlled
  chamfer signature, and tactile-digital controls.
- Use content-authentic research and engineering artifacts.
- Limit the Operator portrait to a theme-reactive ASCII or dithered treatment
  derived from Raihan's own image.
- Use a silent, skippable 2–3 second first-session boot.
- Make Evidence Depth Rail recomposition the dominant visual gesture.
- Treat selected Study B and Study C qualities as bounded sublayers, not extra
  themes.

## Consequences

### Positive

- identity remains coherent across multiple signal profiles and environments;
- visual variation is expressive but testable;
- the OS metaphor is tied to content structure and interaction;
- one token architecture can support later masterbrand integration;
- dark-theme accessibility can be verified against a stable chassis.

### Negative

- the design requires custom composition rather than conventional card stacks;
- theme and semantic-state combinations increase the test matrix;
- variable-font rendering and subsets require browser validation;
- green or amber variants may still invite superficial Fallout comparisons.

### Mitigations

- define primitive, semantic, environment, and component tokens separately;
- test every signal profile against every interactive and semantic state;
- self-host pinned font files and preserve license/version records;
- prohibit scanlines, copied device geometry, fictional telemetry, and mascot
  imitation;
- require equivalent visible navigation for every terminal operation.

## Reversibility

Medium before high-fidelity component production. Expensive after content
templates and Evidence Depth Rail layouts are implemented.

## Affected documents and decisions

- Phase 2 Documents 04–06
- future low- and high-fidelity prototypes
- future design-token and component contracts
- ADR-0003 remains the governing experience foundation
- ADR-0002 keeps the masterbrand decision deferred to Gate 2.7
