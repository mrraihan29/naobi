# ADR-0003 — Adopt the Quiet Field Console Experience Foundation

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Decision owner:** Raihan

## Context

Phase 2 required an experience foundation before selecting colors, typography,
or a public name. Interview 2A established a preference for a silent,
information-rich personal operating environment influenced by field
instruments, disciplined technical systems, archival music culture, and dense
urban infrastructure. The result must remain original and must not reproduce
Fallout, Evangelion, Akira, or generic cyberpunk conventions.

## Decision drivers

- express both AI research and engineering estimation as one credible identity;
- provide dense information without overwhelming the visitor;
- make the OS metaphor perform real navigation and evidence work;
- support quick orientation and deep technical inspection;
- retain professional trust while presenting an uncommon experience;
- keep audio absent and interaction efficient; and
- prevent inspiration references from becoming visual imitation.

## Considered options

### Literal fictional-device interface

Rejected because it would create derivative design, constrain the content
architecture, and risk presenting professional work as a fan experience.

### Conventional professional portfolio with OS decoration

Rejected because the OS layer would be removable and therefore a gimmick. It
would also fail the originality and experience requirements established in
Phase 1.

### Personal cognitive field console

Accepted because it makes action, evidence depth, navigation equivalence, and
Raihan's two professional worlds part of one functional system.

## Decision

- Adopt **personal cognitive field console** as the central experience
  metaphor.
- Adopt **Signal Lab** as the provisional AI Research environment model.
- Adopt **Operations Ledger** as the provisional Engineering environment
  model.
- Adopt **Evidence Depth Rail** with Brief, Method, Evidence, and Artifact
  levels as the signature interaction.
- Adopt **Quiet Field Console** as the primary art-direction territory.
- Limit **Research Broadcast Archive** qualities to time-based field notes and
  the silent external listening archive.
- Limit **Metropolitan Operations Grid** qualities to the global system map and
  controlled moments of scale.
- Use **precise** and **direct** as the only mandatory interface voice traits.
- Keep the website silent; external music requires an explicit visitor action.

## Consequences

### Positive

- both professional environments share a meaningful operating logic;
- visual identity work has a specific, subject-grounded foundation;
- the signature interaction directly supports progressive technical depth;
- references can guide qualities without dictating style;
- interface copy has a narrow and testable voice standard.

### Negative

- the continuous-instrument composition will require more design and responsive
  work than conventional stacked sections;
- structured density demands rigorous hierarchy and content modeling;
- reviewers may initially compare the concept to Pip-Boy or cyberpunk systems;
- Evidence Depth Rail requires content authors to publish structured evidence,
  not only prose.

### Mitigations

- derive every control and label from real content or system state;
- prohibit copied fictional-device components and fake telemetry;
- maintain conventional visible navigation equivalent to terminal actions;
- test density and reading clarity before visual polish;
- use semantic design tokens so the eventual masterbrand can be integrated at
  Gate 2.7 without rebuilding the system.

## Reversibility

Medium during Gates 2.2–2.3. Reversibility becomes expensive after the
high-fidelity component system and content templates adopt the Evidence Depth
Rail.
