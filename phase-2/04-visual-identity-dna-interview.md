# 04 — Visual Identity DNA Interview

**Version:** 0.3  
**Status:** Accepted — Gate 2.2 closed  
**Gate:** 2.2 — Identity System  
**Product Owner:** Raihan  
**Facilitator:** Rei

## Purpose

Interview 2B translates the accepted Quiet Field Console foundation into
specific visual constraints. It does not ask Raihan to design the interface or
select fashionable references. The answers will guide three coherent token and
layout studies before one visual system is selected.

## Locked inputs

- personal cognitive field console;
- Signal Lab and Operations Ledger;
- Evidence Depth Rail;
- Quiet Field Console as the primary territory;
- precise and direct interface voice;
- structured density;
- silent operation; and
- inspiration without imitation.

## Interview 2B — Visual Identity DNA

### 1. Instrument condition

Should the console feel:

- newly engineered and pristine;
- used in the field, with visible age and wear; or
- **a precise core with restrained evidence of use** (recommended)?

Explain what should make it feel authentic rather than dirty or decorative.

### 2. Ambient operating state

Should the primary experience be:

- dark and low-light;
- light like a technical document;
- adaptive between both; or
- dark in the OS shell with a light or neutral long-form reading surface?

The last option is the current recommendation because it preserves atmosphere
without forcing long technical articles into a display treatment.

### 3. Color boundaries

Which colors do you strongly like or reject? Do not choose a complete palette.
State whether either environment has an intuitive color association for you,
or delegate palette development to Rei.

### 4. Relationship between the two worlds

How different should Signal Lab and Operations Ledger feel?

- same chassis with different instruments (recommended);
- closely related but visibly different rooms; or
- almost separate products connected by the Central OS?

### 5. Geometry and controls

Which physical character feels closest to Raihan?

- hard, angular, and engineered;
- compact and softly rounded like a handheld instrument;
- modular panels with mixed geometry; or
- another description?

State whether controls should feel tactile and mechanical, purely digital, or
a controlled mixture.

### 6. Typographic character

Which reading character is more appropriate?

- technical manual and scientific publication;
- instrument labels and machine readouts;
- editorial archive;
- or a disciplined combination of all three?

The recommendation is three explicit roles: expressive system display,
high-legibility long-form reading, and compact data/utility text.

### 7. Real engineering and research artifacts

Which visual materials can you realistically provide later? Examples include
plots, experiment logs, notebook fragments, model diagrams, estimate tables,
mine plans, equipment drawings, revision sheets, or sanitized calculations.
List what may be public and what will require sanitization.

### 8. Portrait and operator identity

Should your face appear:

- prominently in the Central OS;
- only in an Operator Record/About module (recommended);
- as an optional technical or archival portrait treatment; or
- not at all initially?

### 9. Motion intensity

The recommended motion rule is one orchestrated boot/calibration moment plus
brief state transitions, with no continuous decorative animation. Should the
first visit tolerate a skippable 2–3 second boot sequence, or should it be
faster?

### 10. Originality risk

Which controlled creative risk feels most valuable?

- the entire desktop composition behaves as one continuous instrument;
- the global knowledge map behaves like an operational city/infrastructure;
- the Evidence Depth Rail physically restructures the page as depth changes;
- or another risk?

Choose one as the dominant memorable gesture. Other ideas must remain
subordinate.

## Resulting outputs

After the answers are accepted, Rei will produce:

1. three visual-system studies with 4–6 color tokens each;
2. typography candidates for display, reading, and utility roles;
3. shared and environment-specific geometry rules;
4. texture, imagery, iconography, and diagram principles;
5. motion grammar and silent-state rules;
6. compact layout concepts and wireframes;
7. a self-critique against generic portfolio, hacker, Fallout, and cyberpunk
   patterns; and
8. one design-lead recommendation for Gate 2.2 selection.

## Response record — 2026-08-15

1. **Instrument condition:** precise core with restrained evidence of use.
2. **Operating state:** adaptive OS with a black base and selectable signal or
   component treatments such as green and orange.
3. **Color boundaries:** delegated to the design lead.
4. **World relationship:** one chassis with different instruments.
5. **Geometry and controls:** delegated to the design lead using the accepted
   experience foundation.
6. **Typography:** technical manual, machine readout, and editorial archive
   roles may be combined according to page purpose.
7. **Artifacts:** delegated to the design lead according to feature and content
   truth.
8. **Portrait:** limited to Operator Record/About, using a theme-reactive
   instrument or ASCII treatment without copying another property's artwork.
9. **Motion:** a skippable 2–3 second first-visit boot is acceptable.
10. **Creative risk:** Evidence Depth Rail physically restructures the page as
    depth changes.

The resulting visual studies and design-lead decisions are recorded in
`05-adaptive-visual-system-studies.md`.
