## 6. Deferred browser evidence

The following are intentionally Phase 3 launch-blocking evidence rather than
Phase 2 paper claims:

- actual Recursive and Source Serif rendering;
- measured contrast for all signal and future semantic-state colors;
- content-pressure breakpoint confirmation;
- keyboard and screen-reader behavior;
- timing and reduced-motion recordings; and
- visual overlap, clipping, and local-overflow screenshots.

This deferral is bounded by ADR-0008. Feature expansion may not proceed before
the representative browser prototype passes HF-01 through HF-12.
