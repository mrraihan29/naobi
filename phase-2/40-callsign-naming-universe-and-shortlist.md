**Version:** 0.2

**Research date:** 2026-08-15

## 1. Decision requested

This document replaces a question-first naming process with a candidate-first
review. Raihan requested a name that feels like a callsign, may use roots from
ancient languages or civilizations, does not need to contain `Raihan`, and is
selected for identity before it is matched to a domain.

The immediate decision is not a final name. It is the selection of three to
five candidates whose sound and character deserve deeper development. Domain,
handle, and formal confusing-similarity research remains deferred until that
preference signal exists.

## 2. Naming brief

The candidate must:

- work as a name that another person can use to address Raihan;
- feel like a technical callsign rather than a company, fictional faction, or
  decorative project title;
- remain credible beside an AI paper, engineering proposal, GitHub profile,
  CV, conference identity, and professional email;
- hold AI Research, Engineering/Mining, quantitative thought, and personal
  writing without being trapped inside one discipline;
- sound precise, direct, calm, intelligent, and unusual;
- remain human-owned through permanent `by Raihan [professional name]`
  attribution;
- avoid `AI`, `tech`, `labs`, `cyber`, numbers, punctuation, and ornamental
  pseudo-Japanese spelling as the source of distinctiveness; and
- have a truthful etymology note. A coined name must be labelled as coined,
  never presented as an authentic historical word.

## 3. Research method and limits

The exploration used historical dictionary or academic sources to verify each
root, followed by a current web collision screen focused on AI, software,
research, engineering, analytics, and estimation.

This is an identity screen, not trademark clearance. It does not establish
legal availability, ownership, registrability, domain availability, or handle
availability. Those checks are required only for finalists and must be repeated
at the time of decision because current use changes.

The scoring below deliberately defers the 10-point domain/handle/search
criterion from Document 01. Candidates are therefore scored provisionally out
of 90:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Identity fit and truthfulness | 25 |
| Professional authority and trust | 20 |
| Distinctiveness and memorability | 15 |
| Ability to hold both domains and future writing | 15 |
| Pronunciation, spelling, and verbal use | 10 |
| Ten-year durability | 5 |
| **Provisional total** | **90** |

## 4. Recommended shortlist

### 4.1 LIMNAR — lead recommendation

**Spoken form:** `LIM-nar`  
**Construction:** coined name derived from Latin `limen`; `Limnar` is not
claimed to be an attested Latin word.  
**Root meaning:** threshold, doorway, border, limit, or starting line.  
**Provisional score:** **85/90**

Why it fits:

- it describes the masterbrand as the threshold into Raihan's two operational
  worlds without naming either world;
- it feels like a compact callsign and a credible publishing identity;
- it supports the Central OS gateway and two-door architecture without relying
  on the word `OS`;
- it is direct to pronounce in Indonesian and English; and
- its meaning remains valid even if the interface metaphor changes later.

Primary weakness:

- the root story is conceptual rather than explicitly about evidence or
  precision;
- the coined suffix must be owned through consistent use; and
- unrelated uses of `Limnar` exist, although the initial screen found no
  obvious dominant AI-research or engineering-platform identity.

Architecture test:

```text
LIMNAR
by Raihan [professional name]

LIMNAR OS
├── Signal Lab
├── Operations Ledger
└── Field Notes
```

### 4.2 SEMRION — evidence-signal candidate

**Spoken form:** `SEM-ri-on`  
**Construction:** coined from Ancient Greek `sēmeion`, meaning a sign, mark,
token, or evidentiary indication; `Semrion` is not claimed as an Ancient Greek
word.  
**Provisional score:** **83/90**

Why it fits:

- it connects the identity to signals, evidence, observation, and inspection;
- it can hold both AI model evidence and engineering evidence;
- it sounds like a callsign without copying a known fictional system; and
- the initial collision screen was unusually quiet compared with more obvious
  Greek-root candidates.

Primary weakness:

- some people may initially read it as `sem-REE-on` or omit the first `r`;
- the etymology is less immediately legible than Limnar; and
- its slightly synthetic sound needs a restrained wordmark to remain human.

### 4.3 NEMEQ — ancient knowledge-and-skill candidate

**Spoken form:** `NEH-mek`; the final `q` is pronounced as `k`.  
**Construction:** a display form based on the normalized Akkadian `nēmeq`, from
`nēmequ`, attested with the meaning wisdom; broader scholarly sources also
associate the term with knowledge, sagacity, and skilled know-how.  
**Provisional score:** **82/90**

Why it fits:

- the semantic field unites theoretical knowledge and practical craft, making
  it unusually suitable for AI Research and Engineering together;
- it feels genuinely ancient without imitating a famous deity, warrior, or
  franchise;
- the shape is compact, distinctive, and instrument-like; and
- it gives the identity a deeper humanistic layer without sounding academic in
  ordinary use.

Primary weakness:

- English speakers may ask how the final `q` is pronounced;
- it requires a one-line pronunciation rule in the early brand system; and
- `nemeq.com` has appeared in current web infrastructure, so domain work must
  not assume the exact-match `.com` is available.

### 4.4 TEKRION — proof-and-inspection candidate

**Spoken form:** `TEK-ri-on`  
**Construction:** coined from Ancient Greek `tekmērion`, meaning evidence or
proof; `Tekrion` is not claimed as an Ancient Greek word.  
**Provisional score:** **79/90**

Why it fits:

- it maps most directly to the fixed brand promise: what attracts attention
  can withstand inspection;
- it sounds controlled, technical, and suitable as a callsign; and
- it does not privilege AI or Engineering.

Primary weakness:

- it sounds closer to a technology company than a person;
- historic company and event uses of `Tekrion` already exist; and
- nearby root forms such as `Tekmor` and `Tekmar` are actively occupied in
  analytics, software, and engineering, reducing semantic territory.

### 4.5 VITREN — wise-operator candidate

**Spoken form:** `VIT-ren`  
**Construction:** coined callsign developed from Old Norse `vitr`, meaning wise
or intelligent; `Vitren` is not claimed as an Old Norse word.  
**Provisional score:** **79/90**

Why it fits:

- it sounds like a person or operator rather than a corporation;
- it is short, memorable, and visually strong in uppercase or title case;
- it is broad enough for technical and personal writing; and
- its root emphasizes judgment rather than fashionable AI terminology.

Primary weakness:

- `vitren` also occurs as a term associated with a bright coal constituent in
  some languages, which mining professionals may notice;
- the root-to-coined-name relationship is not self-evident; and
- it has a faint pharmaceutical or materials-brand sound.

### 4.6 HAKARU — Japanese measure-and-judgment candidate

**Spoken form:** `ha-KA-ru`  
**Construction:** direct romanization of Japanese `計る` (`hakaru`).  
**Root meaning:** to measure against a standard; dictionary senses also include
judging, inferring, planning, or considering.  
**Provisional score:** **76/90**

Why it fits:

- it connects quantitative measurement with human judgment;
- it is warm enough to be a nickname and precise enough for engineering;
- it can represent the act of inquiry rather than a single output; and
- it is easy to pronounce consistently once heard.

Primary weakness:

- it is a living Japanese word rather than a uniquely ownable coined form;
- it carries more existing names, products, and cultural search noise than the
  top three; and
- the brand would need careful use to avoid decorative or superficial Japanese
  styling. No kanji should be used as an ornament or implied logo shorthand.

## 6. Strong roots deliberately rejected as direct names

The following candidates demonstrate why a verified meaning is not enough:

| Name | Attractive meaning | Rejection reason from initial current-use screen |
|---|---|---|
| `SEKED` | Ancient Egyptian slope/measurement ratio | An active AI estimating and pricing product uses the exact name, and an engineering research/prototyping business uses it. This is an almost exact category collision. |
| `MERKHET` | Ancient Egyptian astronomical timekeeping/survey instrument | Active AI decision-intelligence and precision-timing companies use the name. |
| `TEKMOR` | Ancient Greek sure sign or proof | Active analytics/ML and privacy-proof products use the exact name. |
| `AKRION` | Coined from Greek exactness/precision | Multiple current AI and semiconductor-technology businesses use the exact name. |
| `NOETRA` | Coined intellectual/noetic territory | A major current Japanese physical-AI and foundation-model organization uses the exact name. |
| `CERNO` | Latin sift, distinguish, decide, comprehend | Multiple AI, software, and operational-intelligence companies use the name. |
| `SHIRABE` | Japanese investigate, inspect, or tune | A current AI research-orchestration product uses the exact name. |
| `KIROKU` | Japanese record or documentation | Established AI documentation software and other technology projects use it. |
| `KOTOWARI` | Japanese reason, principle, or order | Current AI/operations-research, spatial-data, and software-framework uses exist. |

These are rejected as public masterbrands, not rejected as cultural or semantic
inspiration.

## 7. Design-lead recommendation

The strongest first preference is **LIMNAR**. It best balances callsign quality,
professional authority, the two-gateway architecture, pronunciation, and
ten-year durability.

The strongest intellectually distinctive alternative is **NEMEQ**. It has the
best semantic union of theoretical knowledge and practical craft, but asks the
audience to learn one pronunciation rule.

The strongest evidence-led alternative is **SEMRION**. It carries the site's
signal-and-inspection character most directly while remaining more ownable in
the initial screen than the obvious Greek evidence words.

The recommended preference set for the next round is therefore:

```text
LIMNAR  — architectural, calm, gateway
NEMEQ   — ancient, intelligent, knowledge plus craft
SEMRION — evidentiary, scientific, signal-driven
```

`TEKRION`, `VITREN`, and `HAKARU` should survive only if Raihan feels a stronger
personal pull toward their sound.

## 8. Next decision sequence

1. Raihan marks three to five names as `like`, `uncertain`, or `reject`, and may
   reject the entire set without penalty.
2. Rei develops the surviving names through address tests, spoken-introduction
   tests, wordmark behavior, masterbrand architecture, and negative-language
   checks.
3. Raihan selects a maximum of three finalists.
4. Only then are domain/TLD, handle, broader organization/product collision,
   and preliminary trademark/confusing-similarity screens performed.
5. A final name, exact capitalization, pronunciation, attribution line, and
   domain direction are accepted in a naming ADR before Phase 2 lock.
