# 17 — Flagship Eligibility and Selection Scorecard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Accepted content-neutral contract; no flagship selected  
**Gate:** 2.4 — Publishing and Flagship Design

## 1. Selection principle

The flagship may come from AI Research or Engineering. It is selected when the
work is ready, not to balance the gateways or fill a launch layout. Visual
potential cannot compensate for weak evidence, confidentiality risk, or absent
artifacts.

## 2. Eligibility vetoes

A candidate is ineligible when any applies:

- core claim or objective is unresolved;
- validation failure blocks interpretation;
- required evidence or artifact rights are unknown;
- confidential material cannot be transformed safely;
- interaction would be decorative or misleading;
- static fallback cannot preserve the conclusion;
- result is Coming Soon or private work in progress;
- authorship/contribution claim is unclear; or
- maintenance exceeds the approved single-author capacity without mitigation.

## 3. Weighted scorecard

Score each dimension 0–5 and multiply by weight. The score supports judgment; it
is not a statistical result.

| Dimension | Weight | 5-point condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence readiness | 25% | claim chain, validation, uncertainty, and limitations complete |
| Explanatory value | 20% | important idea becomes materially easier to understand |
| Interaction necessity | 15% | interaction reveals behavior/evidence static media cannot show as well |
| Originality | 15% | contribution or synthesis is distinctly Raihan's |
| Artifact/reproducibility quality | 15% | inspectable, versioned, rights-cleared artifacts and fallback |
| Identity and collaboration fit | 10% | strengthens professional identity and creates a credible next action |

Recommended selection threshold: normalized score at least 75/100, no dimension
below 3/5, and no veto. Raihan may approve a bounded exception only with written
rationale and mitigation.

## 4. Candidate dossier

For each candidate record:

- content ID and proposed type;
- question/objective and contribution;
- evidence/validation status;
- intended audience and decision value;
- proposed interaction and static fallback;
- artifact and rights state;
- confidentiality state;
- build and ongoing maintenance estimate;
- score with rationale; and
- unresolved blockers.

## 5. Selection workflow

1. Nominate only publication-ready or near-ready candidates.
2. Run the relevant Research or Engineering gate.
3. Apply interaction admission test.
4. Resolve rights/confidentiality blockers.
5. Build a structural prototype with real approved evidence.
6. Score independently before visual polish.
7. Review the highest candidate against launch capacity.
8. Record Product Owner selection in an ADR.

## 6. Current state

No candidate is selected. This is intentional. Gate 2.4 provides the contract;
the actual flagship remains a launch-content decision and may block public
launch if the Phase 1 minimum inventory is unchanged.
