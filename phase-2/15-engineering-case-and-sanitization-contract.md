# 15 — Engineering Case Study and Sanitization Contract

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Gate:** 2.4 — Publishing and Flagship Design

## 1. Publication objective

Demonstrate defensible cost-engineering or project-estimation capability without
disclosing client identity, proprietary database content, protected formula
logic, confidential commercial information, or misleading transformed results.

The public case is evidence of method and judgment, not a substitute for the
client deliverable or Competent/Qualified Person sign-off.

## 2. Minimum case contract

- sanitized project context and engineering objective;
- Raihan's actual responsibility and decision boundary;
- scope, battery limits, inclusions, and exclusions;
- definition maturity and purpose of estimate;
- AACE class/study-stage statement only when supported and current convention is
  verified;
- basis date, currency, location/jurisdiction context, and escalation basis when
  relevant to disclosed figures;
- method by major scope and why it fits maturity;
- WBS or auditable decomposition;
- assumptions, qualifications, data gaps, and unquantified risks;
- source register with visible evidence status;
- calculation/derivation description without proprietary formula disclosure;
- at least one independent cross-check;
- uncertainty/accuracy/contingency basis without false precision;
- sanitization and transformation register;
- conclusions limited to preserved evidence; and
- safe artifacts, rights state, and collaboration route.

## 3. Source-status vocabulary

- **Verified — user supplied:** original controlled project input available to
  Raihan; publication still requires confidentiality review.
- **Cited — external, dated:** identifiable public source with vintage/access
  date.
- **Reference-only — validation required:** directional reference unsuitable
  above its disclosed screening purpose.
- **Unavailable:** no defensible public source; figure or claim omitted.

Every disclosed cost figure, rate, productivity, factor, index, or contingency
input carries one status. Good sourcing does not convert an estimate into fact.

## 4. Maturity and classification

Estimate class follows scope/design maturity and estimate purpose, not desired
confidence or project value. A public case must describe the evidence used to
assign maturity. Exact AACE accuracy percentages require verification against
the current licensed recommended practice or applicable company standard.

When maturity evidence is insufficient, state that classification is not
published. Do not infer a higher class from polished presentation.

## 5. Confidentiality levels

| Level | Meaning | Public action |
|---|---|---|
| C0 Public | already public and rights cleared | may publish with citation |
| C1 Generalized | non-identifying method/context | publish after review |
| C2 Transformed | indexed, normalized, ranged, aggregated, or synthetic replacement | publish with transformation disclosure and re-identification check |
| C3 Prohibited | client identity, confidential quote/price, protected contract, proprietary database/formula, unsafe metadata | never publish |

## 6. Allowed transformations

### Indexed

Express values relative to a disclosed neutral base. Do not expose the base if
it is protected. State whether relationships remain exact or illustrative.

### Normalized

Divide by a safe scale such as capacity or total, only when the denominator and
result cannot enable reconstruction. Preserve units or explicitly state
dimensionless presentation.

### Ranged

Use ranges broad enough to protect confidentiality but narrow enough to avoid a
misleading technical conclusion. State that the range is sanitized, not an
accuracy interval unless it truly is one.

### Synthetic replacement

Replace protected values with a coherent synthetic fixture used only to explain
method. Label every output as synthetic and prohibit comparison with the real
client result.

Transformations must not change the direction of a conclusion, imply an AACE
accuracy range, or fabricate project performance.

## 7. Sanitization register

For every removed or transformed field, record privately:

- source artifact and location;
- confidentiality level;
- transformation/removal action;
- reconstruction or triangulation risk;
- effect on the public claim;
- reviewer and review date; and
- final approval/block decision.

The private register is never shipped with the public site.

## 8. File and media inspection

Before publication, inspect:

- filenames and directory names;
- document properties, comments, revision history, hidden sheets/rows/columns,
  named ranges, formulas, external links, and embedded objects;
- image EXIF and cropped-but-retained content;
- PDF layers, annotations, attachments, bookmarks, and hidden text;
- code history, environment files, secrets, endpoints, and dataset paths; and
- chart labels, hover tooltips, accessibility text, and downloadable source.

Screenshots are not presumed safe merely because visible cells are cropped.

## 9. Estimate evidence test

An auditable public case should allow a qualified reader to answer:

1. What was inside and outside scope?
2. What maturity and decision did the estimate support?
3. Which method applied to each major scope and why?
4. What input source status existed?
5. What assumptions materially drove the result?
6. How was the result cross-checked?
7. What uncertainty and risk remained?
8. What was changed or withheld for confidentiality?
9. What exactly did Raihan contribute?

If sanitization prevents these answers, publish a method note instead of an
Engineering Case Study.

## 10. Publication blockers

- client approval or rights unclear where required;
- client/location/project identity reconstructable;
- proprietary database, quote, rate, or formula recoverable;
- scope or maturity claim unsupported;
- cost figure lacks visible source status;
- no independent cross-check disclosed;
- point precision exceeds evidence;
- transformed evidence changes conclusion;
- hidden metadata not inspected; or
- Raihan's role overstated.
