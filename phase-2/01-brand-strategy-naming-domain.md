# 01 — Brand Strategy, Naming, and Domain Decision

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Accepted — NAOBI masterbrand and `naobi.dev` domain direction  
**Gate:** 2.7

## 1. Brand problem

Raihan needs a public identity that can hold AI research, mining and cost
engineering, interactive technical publishing, professional collaboration, and
personal essays without feeling fragmented or generic. The brand must make
Raihan memorable while ensuring that visual spectacle never outruns evidence.

The former working name `Raihan's OS` remains only as a historical project
codename. The accepted public masterbrand is **NAOBI**, permanently attributed
to Raihan, with **`naobi.dev`** as the canonical domain direction.

## 2. Evidence inherited from Phase 1

### Identity

- serious;
- experimental;
- precise;
- detailed; and
- quantitatively minded.

### Public roles

- AI researcher;
- engineering and mining project estimator;
- technical author and experimenter; and
- potential research or project collaborator.

### Intended emotional sequence

    Amazement → Curiosity → Trust → Exploration → Collaboration

### Trust signals

- methodology;
- professional experience;
- original research;
- source and data transparency;
- calibrated claims and limitations;
- reproducibility where applicable; and
- confidentiality-safe engineering evidence.

### Structural requirements

- one Central OS;
- two primary environments: AI Research and Engineering/Mining;
- one persistent identity and shared system shell;
- professional English as the canonical launch language;
- accessible summary with optional deep technical inspection; and
- a direct collaboration path.

## 3. Preliminary brand essence

### Essence statement

**A personal system for making complex ideas inspectable.**

This statement unifies both professional worlds:

- AI research makes models, assumptions, experiments, and uncertainty
  inspectable.
- Engineering estimation makes scope, quantities, rates, assumptions, and
  confidence inspectable.

It also gives the OS metaphor a functional reason to exist: the interface is a
system for inspecting how Raihan thinks and works, not a decorative desktop.

### Positioning draft

For research and engineering professionals who want to understand the thinking
behind technical work, Raihan's digital environment presents research,
estimating discipline, experiments, and writing through progressive depth,
interactive evidence, and transparent limitations. Unlike a conventional
portfolio, it exposes the method and traceability behind the result.

### Brand promise

**What attracts attention can withstand inspection.**

This is a strategic promise, not proposed homepage copy.

## 4. Attribute-to-experience translation

| Attribute | Must be demonstrated through | Must not collapse into |
|---|---|---|
| Serious | editorial discipline, precise language, evidence hierarchy | corporate blandness |
| Experimental | manipulable models, documented trials, visible failures | random visual effects |
| Precise | units, versions, sources, states, assumptions, uncertainty | sterile information overload |
| Detailed | progressive layers and inspectable artifacts | forcing every visitor through maximum depth |
| Quantitative | meaningful scales, comparisons, equations, confidence | decorative charts or fake telemetry |

## 5. Brand architecture constraints

1. Raihan must remain discoverable as the person responsible for the work.
2. AI and Engineering may have distinct environmental expressions but cannot
   appear to be unrelated companies.
3. The system must remain elastic enough for philosophy, essays, negative
   results, and future interests.
4. The name must not imply an incorporated laboratory, team, or institution
   that does not exist.
5. The brand must remain credible in a paper, CV, GitHub profile, engineering
   proposal, conference context, and browser interface.
6. The identity must survive without animation, sound, or the OS shell.

## 6. Naming territories

These are strategic territories, not final name candidates.

### Territory A — Eponymous personal system

Raihan's name is the masterbrand; `OS`, `Research`, and `Engineering` describe
the environment or sections.

**Strengths**

- builds reputation directly around Raihan;
- supports collaboration and professional searchability;
- remains truthful for a single-author platform;
- can expand into any future subject.

**Risks**

- `OS portfolio` is not distinctive by itself;
- requires an unusually strong identity system and editorial concept.

### Territory B — Eponymous systems laboratory

Raihan remains visible, but the platform adopts a lab/studio-style descriptor
such as `Raihan Systems`, `Raihan Research Works`, or another approved form.

**Strengths**

- creates a stronger publishing imprint;
- naturally holds experiments, tools, reports, and archives;
- can feel more durable than a conventional personal site.

**Risks**

- may imply a team or formal institution;
- can make professional collaboration feel less personal.

### Territory C — Independent conceptual name

The platform receives a symbolic name and identifies Raihan as its author or
founder.

**Strengths**

- greatest potential distinctiveness;
- can support a highly original visual and verbal world.

**Risks**

- weakens immediate association with Raihan;
- creates an additional brand to explain and maintain;
- may reduce name-based discoverability and professional trust.

## 7. Accepted masterbrand architecture

Territory C, an independent conceptual name with permanent human attribution,
is accepted:

```text
NAOBI                              masterbrand and callsign
by Raihan [professional name]      permanent accountable authorship

NAOBI OS                           browser/interface descriptor
├── Signal Lab                     AI Research environment
├── Operations Ledger              Engineering/Mining environment
└── Field Notes                    shared journal and personal writing
```

NAOBI is inspired by the Japanese concept of correcting distortion and
restoring a defensible state. It names a personal publishing and professional
identity, not a company, religious institution, laboratory, or team. Raihan's
professional name remains the author, rights holder, contact identity, and
structured-data Person entity.

## 8. Naming evaluation model

Every shortlisted name will be scored, then reviewed qualitatively. Scores aid
discipline; they do not replace Product Owner judgment.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Identity fit and truthfulness | 25% |
| Professional authority and trust | 20% |
| Distinctiveness and memorability | 15% |
| Ability to hold both domains and future writing | 15% |
| Pronunciation, spelling, and verbal usability | 10% |
| Domain, handle, and search viability | 10% |
| Long-term durability | 5% |

Automatic rejection applies when a candidate:

- falsely implies an organization or credential;
- is confusingly close to a known brand;
- depends on a generic `AI`, `lab`, `cyber`, or `OS` trope for meaning;
- is difficult to say, spell, or attribute to Raihan;
- cannot support professional citation or collaboration; or
- requires an unavailable or legally risky domain to work.

## 9. Domain evaluation protocol

Domain searches begin only after Phase 2 has produced a shortlist grounded in
the approved experience and identity system.
Candidates will be checked for:

- registration availability and renewal cost;
- trademark and confusing-similarity risk as a preliminary screen;
- existing search results and undesirable associations;
- readable spelling when spoken aloud;
- email usability;
- social/profile handle consistency;
- redirect and migration options; and
- ownership and renewal controls.

No domain purchase will occur without explicit Product Owner approval.

## 10. Accepted name and domain decision

The Product Owner selected **NAOBI**, pronounced `na-o-bi`, and then selected
**`naobi.dev`** as the canonical domain direction. The full weighted result is
92/100 in Document 41. ADR-0013 records the accepted decision and supersedes
ADR-0002's temporary deferral.

### Domain contract

- Canonical production origin: `https://naobi.dev` after Raihan acquires and
  configures the domain.
- `www.naobi.dev`, if enabled, redirects permanently to the apex origin.
- The initial product remains one site with path-based environments; selecting
  `.dev` does not require subdomains.
- Subdomains are reserved for future independently deployable or operationally
  isolated surfaces. They are not used merely to imitate product complexity.
- Candidate future namespaces include `research.naobi.dev`,
  `engineering.naobi.dev`, `status.naobi.dev`, and `preview.naobi.dev`, but none
  is approved for public use by this decision.
- Staging/preview surfaces require access control and `noindex`; a descriptive
  non-public hostname may be selected during implementation.
- Email and provider-verification DNS records are introduced with least
  privilege and documented ownership/renewal controls.
- Domain purchase remains a Product Owner action. Registry evidence must be
  rechecked at purchase time.

### Attribution and cultural-use controls

- Use `NAOBI by Raihan [professional name]` wherever authorship is not already
  explicit.
- Cite Raihan, not NAOBI, as the human author of research and engineering work.
- Do not adopt `Labs`, `Institute`, `Engineering`, or another team-signalling
  suffix while the platform remains one-person.
- Do not use Japanese characters, kami, shrine objects, or Shinto symbols as
  decorative claims of authenticity.
- Repeat confusing-similarity screening before public launch and before any
  later commercial organization or trademark filing.
