## 2. Locked Phase 2 decisions

### Product and experience

- one personal cognitive field console rather than a generic portfolio;
- one Central OS with equal AI Research and Engineering/Mining gateways;
- one shared chassis with distinct environment instruments;
- functional terminal and conventional visual equivalence;
- boot, environment/theme switching, reading mode, interactive flagship, and
  Evidence Depth Rail as signature capabilities; and
- desktop-first full experience with simpler but complete core mobile access.

### Identity and masterbrand

- masterbrand and callsign: **NAOBI**;
- pronunciation: `na-o-bi`;
- permanent attribution: `NAOBI by Raihan [professional full-name form]`;
- product/interface descriptor: `NAOBI OS`;
- canonical domain direction: **`naobi.dev`**;
- apex-first architecture, with subdomains only for genuine independent
  deployment, security, ownership, or operational boundaries; and
- cultural-use and adjacent-name differentiation controls from Document 41 and
  ADR-0013.

### Publishing and evidence

- content-as-code with typed metadata, canonical URLs, and one primary author;
- distinct Research, Experiment, Project, Engineering Case Study, Essay, Note,
  and Coming Soon contracts;
- progressive technical depth with accessible summaries;
- explicit status, version, rights, provenance, limitations, and relationships;
- confidentiality-safe engineering publication and per-artifact rights gates;
- honest L1–L4 interaction contracts with static conclusions and fallbacks; and
- no fabricated flagship topic or result.

### Visual and responsive system

- Quiet Field Console experience foundation;
- Adaptive Signal Chassis identity system;
- specification-as-code visual baseline in Documents 26–30 and
  `artifacts/design-tokens.json`;
- no audio;
- explicit reduced-motion and accessible-equivalence requirements; and
- first Phase 3 browser vertical slice must pass HF-01 through HF-12 before
  feature expansion.

### Architecture and operations

- strict TypeScript, Astro default-static architecture, selective React
  islands, MDX/content collections, Pagefind, Observable Plot, and Web Workers;
- no launch CMS, content database, application database, queue, cache service,
  search server, or heavy server inference;
- private GitHub repository, GitHub Actions, GHCR immutable image, private
  staging, automatic same-digest Coolify promotion, and digest rollback;
- Cloudflare edge, minimized GoatCounter analytics, Resend inquiry delivery,
  Better Stack external monitoring, and independent encrypted backup storage;
- Turnstile disabled unless measured abuse justifies it; and
- 99.5% availability, RPO ≤24 hours, and RTO ≤8 hours with tested recovery.

### Verification and release

- unit, integration, content validation, E2E, accessibility, visual regression,
  performance, security, SEO, rollback, and restore evidence;
- automated and manual evidence layers;
- failed launch-blocking checks prevent release;
- exceptions require owner, risk, compensating control, and expiry; and
- polished official launch, not public beta.

## 6. Change-control rule

Phase 2 is now a controlled baseline. Future work must not silently rewrite an
accepted decision. Material change requires:

1. identification of the affected requirement, document, and ADR;
2. impact analysis across experience, content, accessibility, privacy,
   security, operations, and testing;
3. a superseding ADR or explicit bounded change record; and
4. Product Owner approval proportional to the impact.

Implementation evidence that contradicts an assumption may trigger a bounded
substitute already permitted by the ADRs. It cannot lower the locked quality or
safety bar.
